          How the leak check works

          Each day is compared with the same weekday in the four weeks before and after it, so a busy Monday is judged against other Mondays. A day counts as clearly high when it beats that normal level by three times the usual day-to-day swing and by at least 35%. Neighboring days that are moderately high join the run. Leaks found in the first pass are removed and the normal levels are worked out again. With hourly or finer data we also look at the four quietest hours of each night, because water that never stops flowing at night is the classic sign of a leak.

          The Nexa estimate assumes the leak starts at the beginning of its first day and that water lost before the fix can't be saved. Readings far above anything the meter could carry are left out. Times are shown as written in the file.
